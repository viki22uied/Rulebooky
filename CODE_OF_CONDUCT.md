# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep this project free from harassment for all persons. This
applies regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, skill,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or orientation.

## Our standards

Good conduct includes:

- Use kind and inclusive words.
- Respect different views and skills.
- Accept helpful criticism with grace.
- Focus on what is best for the group.
- Show care for other members.

Bad conduct includes:

- Hateful, sexual, or insulting words and acts.
- Personal attacks and political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Private data shared without clear consent.
- Other acts unfit for professional work.

## Enforcement

Report bad conduct through a private security advisory. Or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. We review each report fast and
fair. We keep the reporter private and safe.

Maintainers who break or ignore this code may lose their role. Other
maintainers decide the result.
